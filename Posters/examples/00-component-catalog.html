1. Primary Colored Card (.card--primary)

Colored cards always place a 36×36px icon beside a dark #242529 title and wrap their body inside an inner white card (.subcard, rounded: 36px, p: 24px) so 3-layer inline emphasis remains crisp.

2. Typography, Bullets & 32px Chips

Every Level 1 .card starts directly with a 32px prose paragraph or inner sub-card below the icon-and-title header—never a small subtitle under the main card title, keeping the top-level visual hierarchy unmistakably clear across every column.

  • 3-Layer Inline Emphasis — Combines regular 32px prose with bold takeaway phrases and inline Geist Mono SemiBold technical identifiers in var(--primary) so complex engineering points scan effortlessly.

  • Two-Tone Square Bullets — 36×36px outer square (#242529, rounded: 12px) with centered 18×18px inner square (var(--primary), rounded: 6px) and 24px horizontal gap.

  • Content-Filled Cards & Subtle Spacing — Every card writes complete sentences to fill its vertical budget naturally, while bullet and card gaps nudge by only a few pixels (±1px–6px) to align flush at 1936px.
